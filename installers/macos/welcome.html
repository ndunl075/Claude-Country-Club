Claude Country Club

A quiet, offline mini golf companion for coding waits.

This package installs the desktop app and its local helper. It never installs coding clients, modifies provider settings, enables login startup, or reads credentials. Connect your existing signed-in local client only after reviewing the connection patch inside the app.

Quit Country Club before an upgrade or repair. Saves and user settings are preserved.

Unsigned CI packages are engineering previews, not approved public releases. Check the publisher and release status before installation.
