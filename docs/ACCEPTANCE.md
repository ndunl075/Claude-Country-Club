## Installer upgrade evidence and remaining scope

The installer now uses a custom transaction section with a native coordinator. It stages and hashes the replacement before a same-volume directory switch, retains the previous application until registration succeeds, and restores fixed product registration on interrupted-switch recovery. The actual 0.1.0→0.1.1 installer test preserves SQLite bytes, the final acknowledged ball state, fixture hook configuration and fixture authentication. A corrupt staged ASAR is rejected with the old executable unchanged. Retrying an installer after an unfinalized swap recovers the old app and its version registration. The real older installer is then refused after a successful upgrade.

Native tests inject rename failure and interruption states, verify registration restoration in unique test keys, and preserve edited replacements or unexpected recovery files. These are scripted failure and process-interruption models; they do not prove physical power-loss safety, disk-full behavior, interactive cancellation or arbitrary enterprise/filesystem compatibility. Keep those gates open. See [implementation and test commands](../installers/windows/README.md).
